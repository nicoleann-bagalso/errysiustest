Her

My intent for these series has to do with the reflection and repetition aspect of what I've seen so far with these glitch processes. The first image is using a rough sketch of a drawing I made in the last two weeks (it's February 15, 2026 today!) and it only slightly distorts the color and the alignment of a piece of the image; I wanted to keep it looking the same, before I went all out. The second image I used the complete drawing alongside audacity, and it's extremely distorted to the point of harsh recognition. The third image I used the first image, and then placed that image into audacity as well and I think it still looks quite cohesive. Like it was the mix of both of these two images that reflect each other, the beginning and the end. I kind of feel like I cheated with using different stages of the same drawing, but I think I quite like how it turned out, and I wish to create a series like this again when I have time with different images.
