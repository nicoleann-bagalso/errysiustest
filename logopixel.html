Fish Rocket !!!

I did multiple renditions, but this is what I've ultimately ended on. It was tough to work with Adobe Illustrator. I have never used Adobe Illustrator before this class, so it was definitely a challenge to learn how to do things and maybe I didn't even do it half of it right. When considering my "brand" identity, while I don't think I can completely do it justice, I tried my best. I wanted to include my nickname of "Nico" somehow, but I also wanted to focus on how to make the N link up with a fish/rocket motif. I actually only added the star around midway through my process because it felt unbalanced and I was almost going to make it a simple circle shape. I definitely hope to revise it in the future. I even experimented in making it more complex, but I don't think I was very successful in my coloring attempts.
