Net Art - Artist Statement


 i love being here with you (2026) is a Net.art piece created with the intention of wanting to learn more about html and css coding, but evolved into a part of me that I am very intimate and passionate about; it is a piece about being tethered to connection. The website itself is not very large, only about 10 different pages are linked to it, but I attempted to place as much meaning to each page connection as possible with that limited amount. Each page also somehow ties back into each other, into a continuous loop, where the only way to stop viewing any piece of its content is to stop viewing the entire website altogether. The Net.art piece is about intimacy, whether it is platonic or romantic, through the existence of technology or otherwise also an absence of it.
